# Philosophy

## Core Identity

Sabdo Pandito Ratu is a **Multi-AI Deliberation Engine**.

Users interact with an intelligent council of AI advisors — not a chatbot.

The interface reflects: **wisdom, transparency, deliberation, trust**.

## Experience Goal

Users should feel they are witnessing an AI council think.

Every visual decision reinforces: *"This is not another AI chatbot."*

## Metaphor

**Council Chamber.** Six advisors (pandito) sit in sacred geometric formation around a wisdom core. Neural connections flow between them. Deliberation is visible, transparent, ceremonial.

## Background Symbolism

The **Gunungan** (Javanese cosmic tree/mountain) represents wisdom rooted in tradition, reaching toward the future. It appears at 2–5% opacity — discovered naturally, never distracting.

## What Sabdo Pandito Ratu Is NOT

- NOT a chatbot
- NOT a landing page
- NOT an admin dashboard
- NOT a SaaS product
- NOT a marketing website
