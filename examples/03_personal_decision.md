## Verdict

> **Proceed with caution — with specific conditions**

Confidence: 72%

### Key Factors
1. 89% startup failure rate is a real statistical reality
2. 8-month emergency fund provides meaningful but limited runway
3. Age and family stage make this a high-risk time for entrepreneurship
4. Potential upside significantly exceeds BigTech career trajectory
5. Critical unknown: co-founder compatibility and product-market fit evidence

### Conditions for Proceeding
1. **Secure at least 6 months of living expenses** beyond the emergency fund before leaving
2. **Validate co-founder compatibility** — spend 2 weeks working together on a trial project
3. **Negotiate a "return clause"** — right to return to a comparable role within 12 months if startup fails
4. **Set a 6-month checkpoint** with clear milestones: 100 beta users or $10k ARR
5. **Spouse must be fully aligned** — make this a joint decision, not individual

### Recommendations
1. If conditions 1-5 can be met: proceed with the startup
2. If conditions cannot be met: defer for 12 months, build savings, validate the idea part-time
3. Request a 1-month notice period at current job to maintain relationships

### Alternatives Considered
- Stay at BigTech (rejected for long-term potential — comfort zone trap)
- Join later-stage startup (viable middle ground — less risk, less upside)
- Part-time consulting + startup (incompatible with CTO role requirements)
