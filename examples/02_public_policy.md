## Verdict

> **Proceed with recommended course of action**

Confidence: 82%

### Key Factors
1. Strong public support (78% consumer favorability)
2. Legal precedent exists (GDPR, EU AI Act)
3. Compliance costs are significant but manageable (2-5% of AI budget)
4. Long-term trust benefits outweigh short-term compliance burden
5. Risk of "transparency theater" must be addressed through enforcement

### Recommendations
1. Implement phased rollout over 24 months (not 18) to reduce compliance burden
2. Establish a clear enforcement framework with meaningful penalties for non-compliance
3. Include a "compliance in good faith" provision for small businesses (<10 employees)
4. Create a public registry of AI system disclosures
5. Mandate annual independent audits for high-risk AI systems
6. Include a sunset clause — review effectiveness after 3 years

### Alternatives Considered
- Voluntary disclosure framework (rejected — low compliance without mandate)
- Industry self-regulation (rejected — conflicts of interest)
- No action (rejected — existing opacity harms consumers)
