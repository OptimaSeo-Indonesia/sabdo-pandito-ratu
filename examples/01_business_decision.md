## Verdict

> **Proceed with caution — monitor key risk factors**

Confidence: 78%

### Key Factors
1. B2B market growing 34% YoY in SEA EdTech
2. Current B2C unit economics below benchmark
3. 70% pivot failure rate is a real risk
4. Phased approach reduces downside exposure

### Recommendations
1. Validate B2B demand with 10 customer interviews before committing resources
2. Run a 3-month pilot with 3 beta customers while maintaining B2C operations
3. Set clear go/no-go metrics: 5 B2B LOIs or $50k ARR within 6 months
4. Establish a 6-month review checkpoint with predefined exit criteria
5. Provide 3-month transition notice to affected contractors

### Alternatives Considered
- Full immediate pivot (rejected — too risky per Devil's Advocate)
- Maintain pure B2C (rejected — churn rate unsustainable per Economic)
- Hybrid dual-track (selected — balanced approach)
